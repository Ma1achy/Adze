# Vectorised regime A — results

Unattended overnight run. Appended as each task completes.

Checkpoints: `denoiser_debug_d16.pt` (vectorised, seed 0). The pre-vectorisation
M4 model is preserved at `denoiser_debug_d16_m4baseline.pt`.

## Task 1 — block-conditional truth after vectorised training

**Seed 0, 20k steps x batch 256, nfe 32, 500 traces.**

| | well-formed | true |
|---|---|---|
| naive-trained (M4 baseline) | 85.3% | **6.6%** |
| **vectorised** | **95.0%** | **29.4%** |
| unconditional single-block | 95.0% | 38.6% |
| noise floor | 82.6% | 6.7% |

Truth goes from *at the floor* to 4.4x the floor. The naive training algorithm
was the dominant term in the M4 failure.

### Per block — not flat

| block | well-formed | true |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 99.2% | **61.0%** |
| 1 | 99.4% | 47.6% |
| 2 | 96.2% | 29.2% |
| 3 | 96.8% | 34.0% |
| 4 | 93.8% | 19.8% |
| 5 | 91.0% | 8.6% |
| 6 | 88.4% | **5.6%** |

This is the third shape: **decent early, collapsing at b5-b6**. An 11x spread
across block index. So the B=5 data-starvation diagnostic is triggered.

**Two things worth flagging immediately.**

1. **b0 (61.0%) and b1 (47.6%) BEAT the pooled unconditional model (38.6%).**
   b0 is the unconditional task — no prefix — so it should be the unconditional
   model's equal, not its superior. The likely reason is that the unconditional
   model pools every block position into one distribution, and block 0 steps do
   not look like block 5 steps; pooling heterogeneous positions makes the target
   harder, not easier. If so, 38.6% was itself a depressed reference and the real
   comparison is per-position.

2. The per-block **gate** losses after vectorisation are flat (b1-b6 all
   0.56-0.82%) while per-block **truth** spans 5.6-61.0%. That is the third
   independent instance in this project of regression loss and sample quality
   moving differently, and the first where they disagree about *which blocks are
   fine*.

## Task 2 — re-measure, retire, or keep

All measured on the vectorised seed-0 model, nfe 32, against the same numbers
taken on the naive-trained model.

### Retired — these were symptoms of undertraining

| diagnostic | naive-trained | vectorised | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| live-dim variance ratio (gen/real) | 0.831 | **0.992** | gone |
| total variance ratio | 0.837 | **1.000** | gone |
| shell under-norm at t=0 (nfe 50) | -7.8% | — (variance now exact) | gone |

The sampler now reproduces the marginal's second moment essentially exactly.
Both of these were load-bearing in my "the flow cannot hit the target" story and
neither survives. The 1b ablation had already shown the shrinkage was not causal
— decoder truth at the measured 0.912 amplitude was 95.8% against 97.5% — so
this is the second, independent reason to drop it.

Dead-dim ratio moved the other way (1.628 -> 3.887, and +279% relative deviation
at t=0), but the absolute variance there is ~0.0003 against ~1.0 in live dims,
and the 1b ablation showed the decoder ignores those directions entirely: noise
at sd 2.0, four orders of magnitude above what the sampler puts there, left truth
at 97.5%. Noise, not signal.

### Survives — properties of the data or the VAE, not the denoiser

| diagnostic | value | why it is unaffected |
|---|---|---|
| latent clustering, NN/mean | **0.280 vs 0.744 null** (ratio 0.377) | property of the frozen VAE |
| prefix explains 2-7% of next-block variance | unchanged | property of the data |
| unconditional 38.6% | unchanged | had no per-position split to starve |
| shift 1.5 > shift 0.5 by 8pp | unchanged | measured unconditionally |

Re-measured clustering directly and it is bit-identical, as expected.

### Survives but weakened — the trajectory departure

| | naive-trained | vectorised |
|---|---|---|
| departure begins | t = 0.500 | **t = 0.375** |
| max live-dim deviation | 22.6% | **13.2%** |

Still real, still in the mid-to-low-t region where the field has to encode
multimodality, but 40% smaller and starting later. This is now the strongest
surviving structural evidence, and it is genuinely stronger than before *as
evidence* — it was previously confounded with undertraining and no longer is.
