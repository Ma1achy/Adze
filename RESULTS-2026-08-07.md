**Converged at iteration 4** (TV 0.0035) and flat to iteration 7 — the residual
~0.003 is sampling noise at n = 20k, not drift. `leaf_iterations: 5` in the config,
one past convergence.

The original sampler's mismatch was **0.5014** — half the mass in the wrong place.

### Two consequences I am flagging rather than burying

**1. The fixed point piles against the cap.** 60% of leaf mass now sits in
300-999, with `MAGNITUDE_CAP = 1000`. Inputs and outputs match, which was the
goal, but they match at a distribution the cap is shaping. A different cap would
give a different fixed point.

**2. Multiplication is nearly gone.** Operator mix over 5000 traces:

| | `*` | `+` | `-` |
|---|---|---|---|
| uniform 1..20 | 27.9% | 35.7% | 36.3% |
| empirical fixed point | **0.5%** | 38.9% | 60.6% |

Products of two large operands do not fit under the cap, so `*` stops being
offered. **This is a real cost of self-consistency at a fixed cap, and it is a
confound on any truth comparison against the old data**: the new task is
addition/subtraction of 3-digit numbers where the old one was a mix including
multiplication of small ones. A truth number that moves has two possible causes.

Step length was checked before regenerating: max 20 tokens against
`MAX_STEP_LEN = 24`, so nothing overflows. Steps per trace unchanged at 4.37.

## TASK 1b — the VAE on the new distribution: GATE 1 FAILS at 20k steps

Retrained 4L x 128w, D=16, 60k traces, batch 256, 20k steps — the settings that
gave 95.65% on the old data.

```
token accuracy          98.61%
exact match             72.43%   <- the gate, needs > 95%
exact match, unseen     71.99%   (31483 steps not in training)
FAIL
```

**The task got substantially harder and this is the first place it shows.** With
19 tokens per step, 98.61% per-token compounds to 0.9861^19 ≈ 76.6% — the model is
getting nearly every character right and losing whole steps to single ones.

**Memorisation is gone, which is the good news.** On the old data 87.1% of
held-out steps appeared verbatim in training and the gate was largely measuring
recall. Now 31483 of ~35k held-out steps are unseen, and gate (72.43%) and unseen
reconstruction (71.99%) have converged to the same number. **The gate now measures
generalisation, because the value space grew ~1000x and there is nothing left to
memorise.**

Diagnosis: budget, not capacity. Held-out exact was still climbing at the end
(66.7 → 69.7 → 72.0 → 72.4) under an annealing cosine LR, and reconstruction loss
was 0.049, not near zero. K*D = 64 floats against ~22 bits of step content is not
a bottleneck. Relaunched at 60k steps — a CLI budget override, not a config edit
and not a threshold move.

## TASK 6 — retroactive re-read: one session-11 conclusion inverts

`scripts/m4_reread.py`, on the ORIGINAL uniform-leaf data and the original
checkpoints — kept precisely for this, which is why the config rename gave the new
artefacts fresh names instead of overwriting.

| arm | RAW true | MATCHED true | inflation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2L, eta=0 — the M4 headline | 29.8% | **5.6%** | 5.3x |
| 2L, eta=1 — the session-11 headline | 46.6% | **10.6%** | 4.4x |
| 4L, eta=1 — the capacity arm | 51.8% | **13.1%** | 4.0x |
| zero-prefix, eta=1 — "the best configuration" | 53.3% | **9.2%** | **5.8x** |

The matched ceiling is 74.5% — the same as the old pooled ceiling, necessarily, since
reweighting a real-data measurement by the real distribution is the identity. So the
honest readings are **8% of ceiling** for the M4 headline and **18%** for the best arm.

### What survives

- **SDE helps.** 5.6% → 10.6% matched, a 1.9x gain where raw said 1.56x. The
  correction *strengthens* this conclusion: raw truth understated it, because the
  ODE arm was inflating harder (5.3x vs 4.4x).
- **Capacity helps a little.** 10.6% → 13.1% matched, consistent with raw.
- **The magnitude cliff.** Untouched — it was always a per-bin statement.

### What inverts

**"No prefix beats a generated prefix" is an artifact of difficulty selection.**
The zero-prefix model had the highest raw truth of any arm measured (53.3%) and
has the **highest inflation factor of any arm (5.8x)**. Under matched truth it
comes *last* among the eta=1 arms: 9.2% against 10.6% for the ordinary
with-prefix model.

It was not better. It was picking easier problems — which is exactly what a model
with no context to constrain it would do, and exactly what raw pooled truth cannot
see. Session 11 reported this as "conditioning on a generated prefix is worse than
conditioning on nothing, and the penalty widens as the sampler improves". **That
statement is withdrawn.**

This independently re-confirms the un-promotion of stage 2, by a second route: the
measurement that promoted it does not survive the correction at all. The
un-promotion had been reasoned from `correct ≈ generated`; now `generated > none`
as well, so there is no exposure-bias signal left anywhere in that experiment.

**Three of the four numbers I reported as headlines last session were inflated
4-6x, and one ordering was backwards.** Distribution-matched truth is not a
refinement of the old readout — it disagrees with it about which configuration is
better.
