## TASK 1b — the VAE on the new distribution: GATE 1 FAILS at 20k steps

Retrained 4L x 128w, D=16, 60k traces, batch 256, 20k steps — the settings that
gave 95.65% on the old data.

```
token accuracy          98.61%
exact match             72.43%   <- the gate, needs > 95%
exact match, unseen     71.99%   (31483 steps not in training)
FAIL
```

**The task got substantially harder and this is the first place it shows.** With
19 tokens per step, 98.61% per-token compounds to 0.9861^19 ≈ 76.6% — the model is
getting nearly every character right and losing whole steps to single ones.

**Memorisation is gone, which is the good news.** On the old data 87.1% of
held-out steps appeared verbatim in training and the gate was largely measuring
recall. Now 31483 of ~35k held-out steps are unseen, and gate (72.43%) and unseen
reconstruction (71.99%) have converged to the same number. **The gate now measures
generalisation, because the value space grew ~1000x and there is nothing left to
memorise.**

Diagnosis: budget, not capacity. Held-out exact was still climbing at the end
(66.7 → 69.7 → 72.0 → 72.4) under an annealing cosine LR, and reconstruction loss
was 0.049, not near zero. K*D = 64 floats against ~22 bits of step content is not
a bottleneck. Relaunched at 60k steps — a CLI budget override, not a config edit
and not a threshold move.

### What survives

- **SDE helps.** 5.6% → 10.6% matched, a 1.9x gain where raw said 1.56x. The
  correction *strengthens* this conclusion: raw truth understated it, because the
  ODE arm was inflating harder (5.3x vs 4.4x).
- **Capacity helps a little.** 10.6% → 13.1% matched, consistent with raw.
- **The magnitude cliff.** Untouched — it was always a per-bin statement.

### What inverts

**"No prefix beats a generated prefix" is an artifact of difficulty selection.**
The zero-prefix model had the highest raw truth of any arm measured (53.3%) and
has the **highest inflation factor of any arm (5.8x)**. Under matched truth it
comes *last* among the eta=1 arms: 9.2% against 10.6% for the ordinary
with-prefix model.

It was not better. It was picking easier problems — which is exactly what a model
with no context to constrain it would do, and exactly what raw pooled truth cannot
see. Session 11 reported this as "conditioning on a generated prefix is worse than
conditioning on nothing, and the penalty widens as the sampler improves". **That
statement is withdrawn.**

This independently re-confirms the un-promotion of stage 2, by a second route: the
measurement that promoted it does not survive the correction at all. The
un-promotion had been reasoned from `correct ≈ generated`; now `generated > none`
as well, so there is no exposure-bias signal left anywhere in that experiment.

**Three of the four numbers I reported as headlines last session were inflated
4-6x, and one ordering was backwards.** Distribution-matched truth is not a
refinement of the old readout — it disagrees with it about which configuration is
better.

### The ceiling moved from 74.5% to 96.5%, and this changes everything downstream

On the old data the unseen-step decode ceiling was **74.5%**. On the new data it is
**96.53%**. The reason is not that the arithmetic got easier — it did not — but
that two things changed at once:

1. **Budget.** 60k steps instead of 20k, worth most of it.
2. **Homogeneity.** The fixed-point distribution is mostly 3-digit addition and
   subtraction. The old data was a mix spanning 1-digit multiplications to 3-digit
   sums, and a fixed-capacity VAE reconstructs a narrower distribution better.

**Consequence:** the decoder is very nearly out of the way now. On the old data,
a generated step in the 300+ bin faced a 58.6% decoder ceiling, so more than
40 points of any failure were unattributable. That excuse is gone — at 96.5%,
almost everything the denoiser gets wrong from here is the denoiser's.

It also means the *comparison* to session 11 is now doubly confounded: different
operator mix, different ceiling, different VAE budget. Numbers from before the
data fix and after it are not two points on one curve.

Well-formed, right sign, roughly the right magnitude, and wrong.

### THE FINDING: the model was never doing arithmetic

**The magnitude-distribution mismatch is now 0.030**, down from ~0.70 on the old
data. The data fix worked exactly as intended — the model generates the difficulty
mix it will be asked for, and `raw ≈ matched` (0.8% vs 0.8%) because there is no
difficulty selection left to correct for.

And with that escape route closed, the arithmetic is at chance.

The old data let a model score 30-60% "truth" by *plausible completion*: with
operands in 1..20 there are few plausible answers, so landing on a valid-looking
step lands on a true one a large fraction of the time. With 3-digit operands,
plausibility buys nothing — the model produces the right *form* and the right
*magnitude regime*, and gets the computation wrong.

Per-bin, this is unmistakable:

| magnitude | gen share | real share | true | ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10-29 | 0.3% | 0.1% | 25.0% | 85.7% |
| 30-99 | 0.8% | 1.6% | 4.5% | 96.5% |
| 100-299 | 11.7% | 13.8% | 1.5% | 96.7% |
| 300+ | 87.2% | 84.4% | 0.6% | 96.6% |

25% at 10-29 (chance ~5%), 0.6% at 300+ (chance ~0.1%). Above chance everywhere,
near ceiling nowhere.

**This reframes every truth number in the project.** Distribution-matched truth on
the OLD data was itself flattered — small operands make plausibility and
correctness overlap. The magnitude fix did not just remove difficulty selection
from the metric; it removed it from the task, and the task turns out to be one the
model cannot do.

### Not yet ruled out: capacity

Every number above is 2 layers x 128 wide at 20k steps — sized for the old data.
On the old data, doubling depth moved matched truth 10.6% -> 13.1%, so capacity
was worth something but not decisive. Whether it is decisive here is a different
question, and the honest one to ask before concluding that the architecture cannot
do this. A 4-layer, 60k-step run is training now; the VAE needed exactly that
3x budget increase to cross its own gate on this data.

**1. Pad blocks were encoded, not `pad_latent`-filled.** `LatentCache.build` fills

### THE RESULT — 500 held-out corrupted traces, nfe 32, eta 1, oracle selection

| condition | exact | **RESULT** | operands | formed | answer | preserved |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 19.6% | 21.2% | 86.6% | 100.0% | 99.6% | 99.0% |
| causal | 0.2% | **1.2%** | 1.8% | 100.0% | 99.6% | 99.0% |
| global | 0.0% | **4.0%** | 0.0% | 96.6% | 99.6% | 99.0% |

**GAP (global − causal): exact −0.2%, RESULT +2.8%, operands −1.8%**

**McNemar on the paired RESULT outcomes: global-only 20, causal-only 6,
chi2(1) = 6.50, p < 0.05.**

### Reading it honestly

**The predicted signature is present.** Global beats causal on RESULT by 3.3x
(4.0% vs 1.2%), significantly, and does NOT beat it on operands (0.0% vs 1.8%).
That is exactly the asymmetry the claim predicts: the corruption changes a
*result*, downstream steps consume the original value, so downstream context
identifies the result and nothing else. Operands are fixed by the question, and
question conditioning does not exist — M5 was skipped — so no context can supply
them and global has no reason to win there. A signal that appeared on both arms
would have been suspicious.

**And the absolute levels make it uninterpretable as support for the claim.**
Regeneration is *far worse than doing nothing*: `none` scores 21.2% RESULT and
86.6% operands, and both regeneration arms destroy that. The model cannot
reconstruct a specific block from its context at all — it emits a plausible,
unrelated, well-formed step. A 2.8pp gap between two conditions that both perform
an order of magnitude below the no-op baseline is a difference between two kinds
of failure.

**I am not calling this evidence for the central claim.** It is a working harness,
a significant effect in the predicted direction with the predicted asymmetry, and
a model too weak for the number to mean what it would mean on a competent one.

### A third problem, and it is structural

**The VAE silently repairs 19.6% of corruptions during encode/decode**, and
preserves the corruption only 34.2% of the time (the remaining 46.2% decodes to
something else again). The VAE was trained exclusively on VALID arithmetic, so a
corrupted step is off-distribution and the encoder projects it onto the nearest
valid step — often the clean one:

```
corrupted '7 - 48 = -31'  -> decoded '7 - 48 = -41'   (the clean step)
corrupted '20 + 38 = 53'  -> decoded '20 + 38 = 58'
```

**The corrupted state is not faithfully representable in the latent space.** M7
starts every condition from a latent that encodes the actual corruption about a
third of the time. That is why `none` scores 19.6% "repaired" on a metric it
should floor at 0%.

This is not a metric artifact to work around — it is a prerequisite the design did
not anticipate. The VAE must be able to represent invalid arithmetic for the
central experiment to be about the denoiser at all.

# Session 14 — the missing controls. The gap tracks the pin.

## The reframe that had to come first

Two readings in the previous session were wrong, and correcting them is most of
the result.

**`none` is not the baseline.** It measures the VAE round-trip, not no-revision.
The VAE was trained on valid arithmetic only, so it projects a corrupted step onto
the nearest valid one and silently repairs 19.6% of corruptions. The regeneration
arms erase the block and discard that free repair. Comparing them to `none` was
comparing them to a different kind of thing.

**The reference is chance, measured as a permutation null** — score a condition's
regenerated block against a *different* trace's target at the same block index.
Measured: **RESULT chance 0.6-0.7%, operands chance 0.0%.** So the committed
causal 1.2% was ~2x chance and global 4.0% was ~6x chance. Causal SHOULD be near
chance: without question conditioning (M5 skipped) and with an unknown leaf
operand, the prefix does not determine the result at all.

## The control the design was built around does not exist

> corrupted block's result not consumed by any later block

**0 of 500.** `_emit` walks the expression tree post-order, so every step but the
root has exactly one later consumer — its parent — and `make_pair` excludes the
root. Structural, not a sampling accident. A test now asserts it so a change to
the generator announces itself.

Two conditions replace it, and the second is stronger than the null it replaces.

## 1. The null, and why it is not zero

Corrupt the ROOT (nothing consumes it) on FULL-LENGTH traces (no padding).
Causal and global give the erased block identical inputs, so the gap must be zero.

| n=500 full-length | exact | RESULT | operands |
|---|---|---|---|
| causal | 4.2% | 7.8% | 15.0% |
| global | 0.0% | 2.2% | 0.2% |
| **gap** | −4.2% | **−5.6%** | −14.8% |

chi2 = 15.19, p < 0.05. Not zero, and negative. Diagnosed rather than assumed:

1. **Not a second noise draw or a forward-attending mask.** The existing stub
   tests already rule both out.
2. **Not the erased block polluting its own context.** Under GLOBAL every context
   block attends to the target, which holds pure noise at t=1; under CAUSAL at the
   root, nothing does. `shielded_mask` keeps the mechanism and removes the side
   effect. Gap under shielding: **−5.6%. Unchanged.** Hypothesis dead.
3. **Crossing conditioning against mask** — `mode` is embedded in the conditioning
   as well as selecting the mask:

   | conditioning | mask | RESULT |
   |---|---|---|
   | causal | causal | **7.8%** — the trained configuration |
   | causal | global | 2.0%  (−5.8) |
   | global | causal | 3.0%  (−4.8) |
   | global | global | 2.2%  (−5.6) |

   **Either departure alone costs ~5pp, and both together cost no more.** The model
   is specialised to regime A's exact configuration, which it saw in 90% of
   training steps; regime B fired on 9.7%.

So the null is a **condition-level handicap from the training mix**, not a harness
fault. It does what a null is for: it sets the zero point, which was never at zero.
At full n (2000, padding included) the handicap is **−2.5%**.

## 2. The headline, replicated and stratified

`early`, n=2000 — the committed condition at 4x the traces.

| | causal | global | gap | chance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RESULT | 1.4% | 3.6% | **+2.3%** *** | 0.7% |
| operands | 1.5% | 0.0% | −1.5% *** | 0.0% |

chi2 = 20.66. The +2.8% at n=500 replicates at +2.3%. The operands gap stays flat
to negative — the predicted asymmetry, since operands are fixed by the question and
question conditioning does not exist.

**By distance to consumer** — more chain to traverse, less gap:

| distance | n | gap | |
|---|---|---|---|

It decays to the handicap. Predicted, and the first thing that would not hold if

**By operand provenance:**

|---|---|---|---|---|

Where both operands come from earlier steps, causal can recompute — and does,

## 3. The redirected pin — the strongest measurement here

Consistent corruption perturbs block b and recomputes everything downstream, so
downstream now consumes the PERTURBED value. The pin is **moved, not removed**.
Because the block index and the perturbation are drawn identically to `early`, the
two conditions are matched at block b and differ ONLY in whether downstream was
recomputed.

| condition | what downstream says | gap vs CLEAN | gap vs CORRUPTED |
|---|---|---|---|
| `final` (root) | nothing — no pin | −2.5% *** | — |
| `early` | points at the CLEAN step | **+2.3%** *** | — |
| `consistent` | points at the CORRUPTED step | −0.5% n.s. | **+1.9%** *** |

chi2 = 21.25 on the corrupted target, global-only 49 vs causal-only 12.

**The gap follows the pin.** Same model, same erasure, same block: when downstream
evidence points at the clean step global wins by +2.3; when it points at the
corrupted step the advantage against clean vanishes and reappears, significantly,
against the corrupted step instead. An effect that TRACKS the mechanism when the
mechanism is moved is a stronger claim than one that merely vanishes without it.

**The sharp cell**, targeted to n=500 by rejection sampling — both operands from
earlier steps, so causal can recompute the clean step while global is pinned to the
corrupted one:

| n=500 both-from-earlier | causal | global | gap | chance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RESULT vs clean | **8.4%** | 1.0% | −7.4% *** | 0.6% |
| operands vs clean | **18.8%** | 0.6% | −18.2% *** | 0.0% |
| RESULT vs corrupted | 2.4% | 1.6% | −0.8% n.s. | 0.6% |

chi2 = 27.57 and 87.10. The two arms are aimed at different targets and the
prediction holds on the causal side decisively — causal recovers the clean step at
14x chance and its operands at effectively infinite times chance. The global side
is only weakly at the corrupted step (1.6% vs 0.6%), because global is weak
everywhere; the handicap is doing the work there.

Directional swing (vs corrupted minus vs clean), by provenance:

| provenance | n | swing |
|---|---|---|
| both-from-earlier | 99 | **+8.1%** |
| one-leaf | 452 | +2.2% |
| both-leaves | 1449 | +2.0% |

## 4. The pad-attention confound is dead

When a trace is shorter than B the erased block attends to PAD blocks under GLOBAL
and not under CAUSAL. Pads carry a constant latent, so they should carry no
information — but they change the softmax normalisation, and that applies to the
committed +2.8% too, not only to the leak check.

Reported for every condition on the pad-free `n_steps == B` subset:

| condition | pooled gap | pad-free gap | n pad-free |
|---|---|---|---|
| early | +2.3% | **+2.9%** | 138 |
| final | −2.5% | −2.9% | 138 |
| consistent vs corrupted | +1.9% | +1.4% | 138 |

The gap is **larger** pad-free, not smaller, in the direction that matters. The
confound is not producing the effect. (n=138 is too small for significance on its
own; the point is the sign and the magnitude, not a p-value.)

## 5. Recalibrated

The null's −2.5% is the zero point. Two independent estimates of the mechanism:

```
early     measured +2.3%   handicap -2.5%   ->  ~ +4.8%
consistent vs corrupted +1.9%   handicap -2.5%   ->  ~ +4.4%
```

They agree. **Additivity is an assumption** — the null is measured at the root, at
a different block position and difficulty — so these are estimates, not
measurements, and are labelled that way in the script's output.

## Which decision-rule branch

> Gap concentrated where the result IS consumed, ~zero where it isn't
> -> the mechanism is confirmed.

**This branch, with one amendment: the gap at the root is not ~zero, it is the
handicap, and the handicap is measured rather than assumed away.** Everything else
holds and more besides — the gap decays with distance to the consumer, is largest
where the prefix determines least, inverts where the prefix determines most, and
moves with the pin when the pin is moved.

The counter-branch — "gap uniform across pinned and not-pinned" — is decisively
excluded: +2.3 pinned-at-clean, −2.5 unpinned, +1.9 pinned-at-corrupted.

## What is still true and unflattering

Both arms remain far below what a competent model would do. Global's 3.6% is 5x
chance, not 50x. The mechanism exists and is exploited weakly; that is a capability
limit, and the per-block magnitude cliff and the 9.7% regime-B share are the two
obvious places to look. The `none` figure of 21.7% is not the comparison — it is
the VAE handing back a free repair the treatment arms erase.

The VAE's inability to hold a corrupted step does **not** confound
causal-vs-global: the corrupted block is erased in both arms, and downstream blocks
are internally valid arithmetic that encodes fine. It breaks the `none` baseline
only. The valid-only prior probably HELPS the treatment arms, consistent with
96-100% well-formedness. Training the VAE on corrupted steps is a baseline
correction, downstream of these controls rather than upstream — not started.

# Session 15 — the mix pilot, and the |S| mismatch

## TASK 0 — the rho / |S| distribution in regime-B training

`sample_subset` draws each REAL block independently at **p = 0.5**, so
|S| ~ Binomial(n_real, 0.5) with the empty set forced to one block. Over the
actual 60k training cache:

| \|S\| | share |
|---|---|
| **1** | **30.5%**  <- the condition M7 evaluates |
| 2 | 32.9% |
| 3 | 23.0% |
| 4 | 9.8% |
| 5+ | 3.6% |

**Mean |S| = 2.24.** By trace length, P(|S|=1) runs 50.4% at n_real=3 down to
6.2% at n_real=7.

So |S| = 1 is NOT absent from training, and the hypothesis cannot be "refine mode
never sees single-block erasure". `sample_subset`'s own docstring says p = 0.5 was
chosen so "the model sees single-block repair (what M7 measures) and multi-block
repair alike".

**The mismatch is real in a different form.** The |S| distribution *within* regime
B is identical at both mixes — the mix share does not touch `sample_subset`. Both
checkpoints therefore see a refine-mode distribution that is **69.5% multi-block**,
and p = 0.50 sees five times more of it. More exposure to a distribution dominated
by |S| >= 2 predicts more specialisation to |S| >= 2, and so worse performance at
|S| = 1. That is the direction measured.

Note against the hypothesis as originally posed: |S| = 1 exposure rose in absolute
terms, from 2.96% of all steps at p = 0.10 to 15.3% at p = 0.50. The claim has to
be about RELATIVE specialisation within the mode, not about absence.

**Sharpened prediction:** p = 0.50 should BEAT p = 0.10 at |S| ~ 2-3 and LOSE at
|S| = 1. If p = 0.50 is bad at every |S|, the checkpoint is degenerate and
interference is back on the table.

## TASK 2 — draft quality. The checkpoint is not degenerate

| checkpoint | share | A-steps | B-steps | MATCHED | raw | formed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| p = 0.10 | 10% | 18057 | 1943 | **28.8%** | 29.1% | 99.4% |
| p = 0.50 | 50% | 10006 | 9994 | **23.9%** | 24.2% | 98.9% |

The p = 0.50 checkpoint drafts at 83% of the reference and emits well-formed text
98.9% of the time. **"The whole checkpoint degraded" is ruled out** — free-running
generation is intact and only the global-conditioned pathway collapsed to 0.0%.

Draft quality DOES differ between the arms, so that difference is not attributable
to the mix without the matched-A control: p = 0.50 received 10k regime-A steps
against 18k. Not run this session (no retraining), so it stays open and
unattributed. The tempting shortcut — "regime A crosses the gate at ~6k so 10k is
plenty" — is a LOSS threshold, and loss is not a proxy for sample quality here.

## TASK 1 — the |S| sweep. The hypothesis is killed; the pilot's conclusion inverts

`regenerate` gained `erase` — a [batch, B] subset superseding the one-hot — so the
eval path can match regime B's training condition. Corrupt one block, erase that
block plus k−1 random REAL others, score ONLY the corrupted block. n = 1000.

| \|S\| | p=0.50 causal | p=0.50 **global** | gap | p=0.10 causal | p=0.10 global | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 1.0% | **4.1%** | **+3.1%** | 0.8% | 2.9% | +2.1% |
| 2 | 1.0% | 3.5% | +2.5% | 0.7% | 3.5% | +2.8% |
| 3 | 1.0% | 2.4% | +1.4% | 0.6% | 1.3% | +0.7% |
| 4 | 0.9% | 1.3% | +0.4% | 0.6% | 1.1% | +0.5% |

**THE |S| HYPOTHESIS IS NOT SUPPORTED.** It predicted p = 0.50 strong at
|S| ~ 2-3 and zero at |S| = 1. Measured: both checkpoints decline monotonically
with |S|, and p = 0.50 is better at EVERY |S|, best at |S| = 1. The decline is
confounded in any case — larger |S| also removes more clean context, which should
hurt regardless of specialisation.

**AND THE PILOT'S CONCLUSION WAS WRONG.** "The mix made things worse" rested on
global scoring 0.0% — but that was the ROOT-corruption null on FULL-LENGTH traces
(n = 500), not the effect condition. Generalising from the null to the model was
the error. On early corruption the p = 0.50 checkpoint is the strongest arm this
project has measured, and its well-formedness is better too (99.5% vs 96.1%).

## TASK 3 — the effect and handicap at n = 2000, both arms

| | p = 0.10 | p = 0.50 |
|---|---|---|
| effect (`early` gap) | +1.9% | **+3.5%** |
| causal / global absolute | 1.1% / 3.0% | 1.1% / **4.6%** |
| handicap (`final` gap) | −2.3% | **−2.2%** |
| recalibrated mechanism | +4.2% | **+5.7%** |
| chance (permutation null) | 0.6% | 0.6% |

**The handicap did not move. The effect nearly doubled.** −2.3 -> −2.2 is
unchanged; +1.9 -> +3.5 is not. Global's absolute rate went from ~4x chance to
~6.6x chance.

This is the additive-assumption test, and it comes out cleanly: the handicap
behaves as a CONSTANT condition-level penalty independent of the mechanism, while
the effect moved on its own. That is what additivity predicts. The recalibrated
figure rises to +5.7pp rather than the raw one rising to meet a fixed +4.8.

## TASK 5 — the distance profile. THE REACH EXTENDS

| distance to consumer | n | p = 0.10 gap | p = 0.50 gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| d = 1 | 1205 | +3.1% *** | **+4.4%** *** (chi2 35.12) |

## Where this leaves the mix

**The mix is a lever for the EFFECT and not for the HANDICAP.** Two separable
things, and the pilot conflated them because only the handicap had been measured
when the first conclusion was written.

Still unattributed: draft quality fell 28.8% -> 23.9% matched, and p = 0.50
received 10k regime-A steps against 18k. The matched-A control has not been run,
so the cost side is not settled. The optimum may be above 0.50 or the trade may
bite before it; two mixes cannot distinguish a point from a plateau.

**Not retired:** the M7 diagnosis of the handicap as specialisation. The 2x2
crossing still reproduces at both mixes, and the handicap's immovability under a
5x change in regime-B exposure is evidence AGAINST the training-share explanation
specifically, not against specialisation in general. The root-null full-length
0.0% at p = 0.50 remains unexplained — at n = 2000 across all trace lengths the
same condition gives 0.7%, so it is specific to the full-length root subset.

## The handicap scales with n_steps — and the mechanism is the reverse of the guess

Predicted: if the handicap is "global gains a wider receptive field containing no
useful information", it should scale with how much irrelevant context global
gains, i.e. with `n_steps`. Free from the existing dumps.

| n_steps | p=0.10 causal -> global | gap | p=0.50 causal -> global | gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 1.3% -> 1.3% | +0.0% | 1.2% -> 0.8% | −0.3% |
| 4 | 1.8% -> 1.0% | −0.8% | 1.6% -> 0.8% | −0.8% |
| 5 | 5.0% -> 1.2% | −3.7% | 4.4% -> 0.0% | −4.4% |
| 6 | 7.8% -> 1.5% | −6.3% | 5.7% -> 0.9% | −4.8% |
| 7 | 6.5% -> 1.4% | −5.1% | 6.5% -> 0.7% | −5.8% |

**It scales, and it is near-identical across a 5x change in regime-B exposure.**
Structural, not trained.

**But the direction of the mechanism is the opposite of the hypothesis.** Global
is FLAT — 0.7-1.5% at every trace length, barely above the 0.6% chance rate. What
moves is CAUSAL, climbing 1.3% -> 7.8% as traces lengthen. Longer traces give
causal more prefix to reconstruct the root from, and it uses it; global gains
nothing from the same prefix.

So the handicap is not "more context with no signal is more noise". It is **global
cannot exploit the prefix that causal can** — a skill deficit in the global
pathway rather than a structural noise floor. Which makes it more surprising, not
less, that 5x regime-B training did not touch it.

Consequence for the recalibration: subtracting the handicap is still the right
correction, because it is a constant condition-level penalty measured where no
downstream evidence exists. But it should be described as a prefix-exploitation
deficit, not as an irrelevant-context penalty.

### The 0.0% closes — as a low rate, not a mystery

`m7_shield.py` rejection-samples until it holds 500 FULL-LENGTH traces, so the
0/500 was not an ~35-trace subset. At the measured full-length global rate of
~0.7% the expected count is ~3.5 and P(0) is about 3%. The independent
full-length subset of `p50_final.json` gives 1/138 over the same condition.
Together they are consistent with a true rate near 0.2-0.5%, so the zero is a low
rate observed at low power rather than an anomaly. Item closed.

| p = 0.10 | 18057 | +1.9% | −2.3% | +4.2% | 28.8% | 99.4% |

**An optimum, not a slope and not a plateau.** The effect rises then falls, and the

**Draft quality falls monotonically** — 28.8 -> 23.9 -> 19.9, tracking regime-A

which is the DiD trade arriving, and it places the optimum at or near 0.50.

**The p = 0.75 handicap shrinking to −0.5 is not good news.** It closed from the
wrong side: causal collapsed 3.5% -> 2.9% -> 1.5% while global stayed flat
1.2% -> 0.7% -> 1.0%. A handicap that vanishes because the reference arm got worse
is not a handicap that was fixed.

### A confound I introduced, and what it exposes

`--seed 1` was used to avoid a checkpoint-name collision, so the matched-A arm
differs from the 20k p = 0.50 arm in TWO ways — 36k vs 20k steps AND a different
seed. A `--tag` would have cost nothing. My error.

| arm | steps | seed | effect | handicap | recalibrated | draft |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| p = 0.10 | 20k | 0 | +1.9% | −2.3% | +4.2% | 28.8% |
| p = 0.50 | 20k | 0 | **+3.5%** | −2.2% | **+5.7%** | 23.9% |
| p = 0.75 | 20k | 0 | +2.3% | −0.5% | +2.8% | 19.9% |
| p = 0.50 matched-A | 36k | 1 | **+1.6%** | −3.2% | **+4.8%** | 27.3% |

**Two p = 0.50 runs give +3.5% and +1.6% on the raw effect.** That spread equals
the whole sweep's range (+1.9 -> +3.5 -> +2.3). The sweep was single-seed
throughout, so **the "optimum at p = 0.50" may be inside seed noise.** A curve
cannot currently be distinguished from scatter, and the earlier claim that the
curve "turns over" is not supported at this evidence level.

This is exactly the standing rule this project already has: *any number that gates
a decision gets reported with its budget and its spread.* The sweep had a budget
and no spread.

### One thing that survives the spread

Recalibrated: +5.7% and +4.8%, a 0.9pp spread against the raw effect's 1.9pp. The
handicap moved with the effect (−2.2 -> −3.2) and subtracting it HALVED the
variance. With n = 2 this is weak, but it is the direction the additive model
predicts, and it is consistent with causal sitting flat at ~1.1% across every run
while global does all the moving.

If it holds up, the recalibrated figure is not merely a fairer number — it is a
lower-variance estimator, which would make it the right headline for reasons
beyond fairness.

## Where the mix question actually stands

- **Regime B's cost at matched compute is small** — 1.5pp of draft quality. Settled.
