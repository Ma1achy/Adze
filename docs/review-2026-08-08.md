## Blocking issues

### 1. §5 still lacks a conditional distance test

The composition predictions are fitted and evaluated on the same records, while
the residual error bars omit uncertainty in the estimated class gaps. Therefore:
the table descriptively shows that provenance composition can reproduce the
pooled profile; the one-leaf d=4 result rejects a hard two-step horizon; it does
NOT establish that "distance adds nothing once provenance is known."

Run either the per-seed conditional regression or leave-one-seed-out composition
prediction. Until then, change:

  "What the decomposition establishes is that distance adds nothing once
  provenance is known"

to:

  "The decomposition shows that changing provenance composition is sufficient to
  account descriptively for the pooled distance profile. Because the same records
  estimate the class effects and the distance outcomes, this is not yet a formal
  test that the conditional distance effect is zero."

Change the heading to: "The apparent distance window is explained by provenance
composition"

Narrow "within a fixed class the advantage persists at every distance measured"
to: "within the one-leaf class, the advantage remains significant at the maximum
measured distance, d=4" — unless every class genuinely has adequate support at
every distance.

### 2. §10 remains false as a general statement

Not true: "In tree-structured reasoning tasks, distance-to-consumer is confounded
with dependency type by construction." Nor: "in any evaluation order its own
consumer is therefore further away."

Tree structure does not determine linear consumer distance. Scheduling and
sibling order do. The result belongs to this generator and linearisation
procedure, not tree reasoning generally. More data also helps wherever cells
overlap; the one-leaf d=4 cell demonstrates overlap. A redesigned generator is
required only for combinations with zero support.

Replace §10's opening with:

  "In this generator, distance-to-consumer is strongly associated with dependency
  type. Its construction and linearisation place different provenance classes into
  different distance distributions. A marginal distance profile therefore combines
  any genuine reach effect with changing provenance composition. Stratification can
  separate the variables where the design has overlapping support; combinations
  with structurally zero support require a redesigned generator."

Replace the slogan with:

  "Replication reduces sampling and training-run uncertainty, but does not remove
  design confounding; stratification or redesign is also required."

"will show a spurious window" becomes "can show a spurious window."

### 3. §11 confuses positivity with dishonesty

Remove: "Excluding those steps is honest; including them and adjusting would be
fabrication." Adjustment is not fabrication. The issue is that adjustment cannot
identify combinations with zero support without additional modelling assumptions.

"No construction avoids it" is too strong. Padding, burn-in, restricting eligible
target positions, or generating longer traces and sampling an interior window all
avoid boundary contamination. The interior-band design is itself one such
construction.

Use:

  "We restrict the distance estimand to a pre-specified interior band in which the
  intended provenance and distance levels are jointly supportable. At boundary
  positions, some combinations have structurally zero support; regression
  adjustment cannot recover those cells without extrapolating beyond the observed
  design. Boundary steps remain in training and aggregate evaluation but are
  excluded from the conditional distance analysis."

### 4. The closest latent-persistence work is still missing

Add Residual Context Diffusion (arXiv 2601.22954) and Learned Relay
Representations (arXiv 2605.22967). Suggested paragraph:

  "Residual Context Diffusion and Learned Relay Representations preserve
  continuous information across successive denoising iterations, demonstrating the
  utility of temporal latent persistence. Our intervention concerns a different
  axis: later positions within the generated reasoning trace. Neither work holds an
  erased position's direct state fixed while manipulating whether visible
  later-position latents derive from a clean or corrupted predecessor."

### 5. Internal contradictions

The draft says all three: the decorrelated generator now exists; "that generator
does not exist yet"; a generator "is what would settle" the question. Resolve to:

  "The decorrelated generator now exists, but no model has yet been trained and
  evaluated on it."

Limitations should say the current generator has SPARSE AND UNBALANCED CONDITIONAL
COVERAGE, not "no distance range is available at fixed provenance" — the reported
one-leaf range disproves that literal statement.
